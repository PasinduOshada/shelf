## FFmpeg (ffprobe), GPL-3.0

The Windows build of Shelf includes `ffprobe.exe` from the FFmpeg project
(gyan.dev "essentials" build, configured with `--enable-gpl --enable-version3`).
It is a separate program that Shelf runs to read a video file's resolution,
length and languages; Shelf does not link against it.

ffprobe is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 (see
`licenses/GPL-3.0.txt`). Its complete corresponding source is published by the
FFmpeg project at <https://ffmpeg.org/download.html> and by the build's author at
<https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/>. On request, the maintainer of Shelf will
also provide the source used for the bundled build.

To build Shelf without it, remove the `@ffprobe-installer/win32-x64` optional
dependency: everything except the file-details feature works unchanged.
